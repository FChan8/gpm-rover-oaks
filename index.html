Every conversation, same five plays.

1 · All-in, in writing. One number that includes tax, freight, setup, dealer fees, delivery to Fayetteville, and rear ballast. Dealers hide $800–1,500 in the gaps.

2 · Cash is the discount. On the KIOTIs, the “0%” price runs $2,500–3,900 above the cash price. If paying cash, negotiate the cash number and ignore the payment talk.

3 · Same-week competing quotes. Tatum (KIOTI), Springdale Tractor Co. (Kubota), Bobcat of NWA — all within 30 minutes. Get three written numbers in one week and show them to each other.

4 · Commercial terms named. Business-use warranty, loader warranty, field service, parts lead time — written down before money moves.

5 · Demo before dollars. Jack drives it — pedals, loader raise/curl/dump, full-lock turns, PTO, hitch, parking brake — before any deposit.

6 · The calendar is leverage. KIOTI’s cash-back/0% window and Bad Boy’s 0% programs end Aug 31; Kubota’s $3,000 rebate and Bobcat’s free-loader offer end Sept 30. Dealers want August deliveries on 2025 stock — a cash buyer who can close this week is holding the cards.

Before Jack travels to a dealer

Pass/fail on the exact machine — not the model name.

  1. Stock The exact serial/stock number is physically on the lot and the online price still applies.
  2. Working package Loader model, bucket width, quick-attach type, tires, rear ballast and any third function — named in writing.
  3. All-in price Cash and financed totals include tax, freight, setup, fees, delivery, ballast.
  4. Commercial terms Business-use warranty, loader warranty, pickup/field service, parts lead time — written.
  5. Site fit Bucket, tractor and ROPS clear the gate, corridor, turn, storage opening and overhead.
  6. Jack demo Forward/reverse, loader raise/curl/dump, full-lock turns, PTO, three-point, parking brake.

Tax, without surprises

Fayetteville’s full rate is 9.75% — 6.50% state + 1.25% Washington County + 2.00% city. Every “out the door” estimate above uses the full rate, so surprises can only be good ones.

Business rebate worth ~$700: Arkansas lets a business claim back the local (city + county) tax on the portion of a single qualifying business purchase above $2,500 — the 6.5% state share is never capped. On a $24,000 machine that’s roughly $700 back. It doesn’t come from the dealer: file DFA Form ET-179A (or take the credit on the business’s monthly excise report) within one year of purchase, with the itemized invoice attached.

Estimates exclude ballast, delivery, dealer fees, insurance and attachments. Inventory and prices move — refresh the exact stock number before travel.
