Every conversation, same six plays.

1 · All-in, in writing. Tax, freight, setup, fees, delivery, ballast — and loader lift quoted at full height with the actual bucket, so brands compare honestly.

2 · Cash is the discount. KIOTI “0%” prices run $2,500–3,900 over cash. Negotiate the cash number.

3 · Three quotes, one week. Tatum, Springdale Tractor Co., Bobcat of NWA — all within 30 minutes. Show them to each other.

4 · Commercial terms in writing. Business-use warranty, loader warranty, field service, parts lead time.

5 · Demo before dollars. Pedals, loader, full-lock turns, PTO, hitch, parking brake — before any deposit.

6 · The calendar is leverage. KIOTI & Bad Boy end Aug 31; Kubota & Bobcat end Sept 30. A cash buyer closing this week holds the cards.

Before Jack travels to a dealer

Pass/fail on the exact machine — not the model name.

  1. Stock The exact serial/stock number is physically on the lot and the online price still applies.
  2. Working package Loader model, bucket width, quick-attach type, tires, rear ballast and any third function — named in writing.
  3. All-in price Cash and financed totals include tax, freight, setup, fees, delivery, ballast.
  4. Commercial terms Business-use warranty, loader warranty, pickup/field service, parts lead time — written.
  5. Site fit Bucket, tractor and ROPS clear the gate, corridor, turn, storage opening and overhead.
  6. Jack demo Forward/reverse, loader raise/curl/dump, full-lock turns, PTO, three-point, parking brake.

Tax, without surprises

Fayetteville’s full rate is 9.75% (6.5% state + 1.25% county + 2% city). Every estimate above uses it — surprises can only be good.

Business rebate, ~$700 on a $24k machine: Arkansas refunds the local tax above $2,500 per business invoice. File DFA Form ET-179A within one year, invoice attached.

Estimates exclude delivery, fees, insurance, attachments. Prices move — confirm the stock number before travel.
